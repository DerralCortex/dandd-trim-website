Who we are

A family crew, twenty years in

Derral Foster started D and D in 2004. Today it's a father-and-sons company out of Post Falls — the same last name on the trucks and on the work.

Most weeks we're trimming new homes for the region's builders. That production pace is why our cuts are clean and our schedules hold. The custom work — mantles, built-ins, ceilings — is where we get to slow down and show off a little.

We show up when we say we will, keep the site clean, and stand behind the work. That's the whole pitch.
